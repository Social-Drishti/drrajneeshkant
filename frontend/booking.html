Quick Answers

Booking FAQ

How soon will I get a confirmation?

We confirm every appointment by phone within 2 hours during clinic hours (Monday to Saturday, 9 AM – 7 PM).

Can I reschedule or cancel my appointment?

Yes. Just call or message us on WhatsApp at least a few hours in advance and we'll move your slot at no extra charge.

Do I need a doctor's referral to book?

No referral is required. If you have reports, X-rays or prescriptions from another doctor, bring them along for a more complete picture.

What are your working hours?

The Mumbai branch runs Monday to Saturday from 9 AM – 7 PM (Sunday by appointment). The Patna branch runs Monday to Saturday from 10 AM – 6 PM.
