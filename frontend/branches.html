Quick Answers

Branches FAQ

Do both branches offer the same treatments?

Yes. Both the Mumbai and Patna clinics follow the same advanced assessment protocols and treatment standards, so you get consistent, high-quality care no matter which location you visit.

How do I book at a specific branch?

Use the online booking form and select your preferred location, or message us on WhatsApp with the branch name. We will confirm your slot on the same day.

Are walk-in visits allowed?

We accept walk-ins when slots are available, but booking in advance is recommended to avoid waiting. The Mumbai branch is open Monday to Saturday, and the Patna branch is open Monday to Saturday with Sunday closed.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Carry a photo ID, any previous X-rays, MRI reports or prescriptions, and wear loose, comfortable clothing so we can perform a full movement assessment.
