Understanding Lower Back Pain & Chiropractic Treatment

Lower back pain is one of the most common health complaints worldwide and a leading cause of missed work and disability. The good news is that the majority of cases respond well to non-surgical, conservative treatment.

Common Causes of Lower Back Pain

  • Muscle strain from poor lifting posture or sudden movements
  • Prolonged sitting and desk work that weakens core muscles
  • Herniated or bulging discs compressing spinal nerves
  • Sciatica and nerve root irritation
  • Postural imbalance and spinal misalignment

How Chiropractic Care Helps

Chiropractic treatment focuses on identifying and correcting the root cause rather than masking symptoms. Through gentle spinal adjustments, traction, and targeted rehabilitation exercises, pressure on nerves is reduced, spinal alignment is restored, and the surrounding muscles are strengthened to prevent recurrence.

Most patients experience significant improvement within a few sessions, with a customized care plan designed for long-term wellness and injury prevention.

5 Effective Exercises To Relieve Sciatica Pain Naturally

Sciatica refers to pain that radiates along the path of the sciatic nerve — from the lower back down through the buttock and leg. These expert-approved exercises can help reduce sciatic pain and improve spinal mobility when done consistently.

1

Knee-to-Chest Stretch: Lie on your back, bring one knee to your chest, hold 2030 seconds, then switch sides to ease lower back tension.

2

Seated Piriformis Stretch: Sit with one ankle crossed over the opposite knee and gently lean forward to stretch the piriformis muscle.

3

Cat-Cow Stretch: On all fours, alternate between arching and rounding the back to improve spinal flexibility.

4

Child's Pose: Kneel and fold forward with arms extended to gently lengthen the lower back and relax the spine.

5

Glute Bridge: Lying on your back with knees bent, lift the hips to strengthen the glutes and support the lower spine.

Always perform exercises gently and stop if pain increases. For a personalized plan addressing the root cause of your sciatica, consult Dr. Rajneesh Kant.
